# EDF6VR 3.2.15

## New

- **Snap turn** (off unless you turn it on): on foot, pushing the right stick sideways turns you 45, 90, 135 or 180 degrees at once instead of smoothly. Choose it under **VR setting** in the Start menu, where it applies at once, or with the slider in EDF6 VR setting.exe.

## Online

- Nix and Eiren with vehicle hand aim: other players now see your cannon, grenade, rocket, missile and laser cannon shots leave the way your arm points. Before, they left along the machine's own aim, up to 20 degrees off. Machine guns, gatlings and flame throwers are not changed yet.

Still bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.12**.

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
