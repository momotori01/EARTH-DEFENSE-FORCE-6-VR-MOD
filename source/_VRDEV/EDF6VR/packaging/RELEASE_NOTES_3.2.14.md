# EDF6VR 3.2.14

## Fencer: two shields

- Each shield now guards the way its own hand points, with its own width. Before, the two made one guard straight ahead, as wide as both together.
- Only a shield that takes a hit blocks it and loses durability. Before, every block cost both shields the full damage, so they broke together.
- A shield bash goes the way its own shield faces, each hand on its own.
- The shield you see turns with its guard, at the Fencer's turning speed, so it shows where it guards.
- The left shield no longer turns upside down or jumps to the right hand, and a shield pointed straight up no longer flips over.
- Both weapons no longer jump out of your hands for a moment now and then.

Still bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.12**.

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
