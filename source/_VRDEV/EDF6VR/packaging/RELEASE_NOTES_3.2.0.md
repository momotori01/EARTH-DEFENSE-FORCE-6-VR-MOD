# EDF6VR 3.2.0

## New

- The Start (pause) menu has **VR setting**: change VR settings without leaving the game. They apply at once. It covers scope zoom, air-strike camera, stick up / down buttons, recoil, shot vibration, aim mark size, compact HUD, desktop mirror, sight line thickness and vehicle hand aim. The menu is in Japanese when the game is in Japanese, and in English otherwise.
- Air Raider: the air-strike camera can be shown in VR. Choose it under **Air-strike camera** in EDF6 VR setting.exe: **2D** (a flat screen, light; the default) or **VR** (the frame rate drops).
- EDF6 VR setting.exe has **Stick up / down buttons**: it turns the right stick's up (Y) and down (L3) on foot on or off.

## Changes

- The right stick's up (Y) and down (L3) now only answer a push close to straight up or straight down, so turning no longer presses them by mistake.
- Air Raider: the three-panel screen of the laser guide weapons now shows one large zoom picture, not the same picture three times.

## Fixes

- Missions 1 and 116: the tutorial could not go on in VR unless VR was turned off with F11. This is fixed.
- Fencer: the aim line of the heavy and scatter mortars pointed down after a shot. This is fixed.

Still bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.9**.

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
