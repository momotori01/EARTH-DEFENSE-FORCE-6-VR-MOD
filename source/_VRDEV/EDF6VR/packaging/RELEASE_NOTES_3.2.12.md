# EDF6VR 3.2.12

## Fencer

- Shoulder mortars: other players now see your shells land where you see them land. From the second shot of a run on, they used to explode somewhere else on their screens.
- Reflectors: the reflecting field now goes the way the shield faces, so it turns back attacks such as the Neiker's flames.
- A dash or jump from the left hand's weapon now goes the way you move, not the way the left hand points.

Now bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.11** (plays together with 1.6.6 to 1.6.10). Its changes are in README_EDF6MultiSlot.txt in the ZIP.

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
