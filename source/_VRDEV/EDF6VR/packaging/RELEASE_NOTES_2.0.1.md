# EDF6VR 2.0.1

**Fixes**

- Brute door gunner: easier to control.
  - The gun starts pointing straight out of the door.
  - Stick up and down are inverted in this seat.
  - Held down, the barrel no longer stops at straight down: it swings through to the other side. Let go of the stick or change direction, and it steers as usual again.
  - The right-hand seat's screens are no longer mirrored.

Everything else is as in 2.0.0. The aim keeps the game's own speed and weight. Updating from 2.0.0 is a straight overwrite; your settings are kept.
