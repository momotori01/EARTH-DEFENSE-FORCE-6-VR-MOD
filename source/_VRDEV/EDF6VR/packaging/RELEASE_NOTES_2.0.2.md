# EDF6VR 2.0.2

**Fixes**

- Tanks (Blacker, Varias, EMC, Railgun, Titan, Kebler): the direction you drive is taken from the hull again. Pushing the stick forward drives the tank forward, and turning the turret no longer changes where it goes.

**Included**

- EDF6MultiSlot 1.5.7: a more detailed log when the game crashes, to track down a rare host crash when a mission starts with 5+ players. Rooms and joining are unchanged. See `README_EDF6MultiSlot.txt`.

Everything else is as in 2.0.1. Updating from 2.0.x is a straight overwrite; your settings are kept.
