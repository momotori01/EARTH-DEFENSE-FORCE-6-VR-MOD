# EDF6VR 3.2.19

## Fixes

- **Online with five or more players:** the game no longer freezes when a group of flying enemies (dragons and the like) arrives along its route. A small group raised for the larger team never finished appearing, and larger groups fell back to their old size. **Everyone in the room needs EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.15.**
- If the OpenXR runtime named by the `XR_RUNTIME_JSON` setting cannot be used, EDF6VR now starts with the one registered on the PC (usually SteamVR).

Now bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.15** (plays together with 1.6.12 to 1.6.14).

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
