# EDF6VR 3.2.6

## Fixes

Fencer:
- Blades and the Power Blade: the attack now goes where that hand aims. It could leave about 25 degrees off.
- A left-hand weapon that carries you forward (Blast Twin Spears, Vibro Roller and the like) now moves the way the left hand points, not the right.
- Arm Hound: it now sits on the forearm, with the hand inside it.
- Arm Hound, Jackhammer and Blast Twin Spears in the left hand are now the right way round.

Still bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.9**.

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
