# EDF6VR 3.2.10

## Fixes

- The Eiren's arms shook while its Electromagnetic Laser Cannons fired. These lasers and the flame throwers (the Depth Crawler's Incinerators, the Nix's Combat Burner) no longer recoil.
- In a vehicle, the cockpit monitors changed size when a radio subtitle appeared.

Still bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.9**.

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
