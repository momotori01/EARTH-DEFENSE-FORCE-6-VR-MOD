# EDF6VR 3.2.21

## Fixes

- **The Eiren's Electromagnetic Laser Cannons no longer show a second beam below the real one.** With the arms aimed by the controller, the laser's large muzzle flash stayed on the game's own barrel, 15 to 20 degrees under where the beams went, and looked like a second beam. The muzzle flashes of the arm guns on the Nix-type machines now follow the arm.

EDF6MultiSlot is unchanged (1.6.16).

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
