# EDF6VR 2.0.3

**Fixes**

- Fencer: spear thrusts go where the spear points. Since 2.0.0 the thrust followed the game's slower aim, so it missed to the side of the spear whenever the hand had just moved.

Everything else is as in 2.0.2, including EDF6MultiSlot 1.5.7. Updating from 2.0.x is a straight overwrite; your settings are kept.
