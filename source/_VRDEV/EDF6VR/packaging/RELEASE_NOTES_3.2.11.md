# EDF6VR 3.2.11

## Fencer

- The aim now follows small hand movements, and the right-hand weapon no longer trembles while you hold still.
- The aim no longer stops while your right hand is beside your head.
- The dash and jump boosters stay on your back instead of swinging into view.

## Wing Diver

- The white flight trails now start from the wings and no longer cross the middle of the view while flying forward.

## All classes

- With the left controller beside a temple, the left stick click opens the menu (Start).

Now bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.10** (plays together with 1.6.6 to 1.6.9). Its changes are in README_EDF6MultiSlot.txt in the ZIP.

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
