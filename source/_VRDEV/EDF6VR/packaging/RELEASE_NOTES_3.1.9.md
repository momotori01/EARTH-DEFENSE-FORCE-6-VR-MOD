# EDF6VR 3.1.9

## Fixes

- When several players rode vehicles, the fifth player and later could not get in. This is fixed.

Now bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.8** (plays together with 1.6.6 and 1.6.7).

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
