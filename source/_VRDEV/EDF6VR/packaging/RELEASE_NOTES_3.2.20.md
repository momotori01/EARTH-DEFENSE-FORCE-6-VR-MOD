# EDF6VR 3.2.20

## Fixes

- **Online with five or more players:** the extra enemies of a flying group that arrives along a fixed route no longer appear heading the wrong way (high in the sky, back toward where the group came from). The game lines such a group up along its route, and the longer line of a larger team ran past the stretch the mission sets out for it and folded back. The group keeps its larger size and is now spaced more closely, so it fits the mission's own line. Seen in *Maximum Power*, *Aliens Attack I* (seven or more players) and DLC2 *LV S-3 Type 3x3*. **Everyone in the room should have EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.16.**

Now bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.16** (plays together with 1.6.12 to 1.6.15).

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
