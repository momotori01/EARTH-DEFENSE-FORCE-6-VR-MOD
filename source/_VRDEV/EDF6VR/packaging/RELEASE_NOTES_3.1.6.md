# EDF6VR 3.1.6

## New

- **When you fire your two weapons in different directions, other players now see them go in those directions too.** Other players see every shot this way when they also have EDF6MultiSlot (bundled with this mod).
- **HD textures can be turned off and on in EDF6 VR setting.exe without deleting them, to compare them with the game's own pictures.**

## Fixes

- Online with five or more players, a player with a poor connection could make several players drop out. This is now countered. You can no longer join 8-player rooms made with older versions. (4-player rooms work as before.)
- Online, the pause menu and the retry choices after the team is wiped out are now shown on the flat screen, without holes cut in them.
- Clear Loot: items are also collected in missions that end on a white or black text screen.

Now bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.6**.

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
