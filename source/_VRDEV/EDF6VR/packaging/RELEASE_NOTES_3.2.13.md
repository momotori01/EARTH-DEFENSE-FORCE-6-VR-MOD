# EDF6VR 3.2.13

## Online (EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.12)

- Blown-away enemies are now kept in sync only while a hit has them flying or knocked down, until they get up. 1.6.11 also did it for the enemies' own jumps and boosts, which doubled the host's network traffic in missions with many spiders.
- A room that mixes 1.6.11 and 1.6.12 leaves enemies as the game has them. Everyone in the room needs 1.6.12 for this.

Now bundles **EDF6MultiSlot 1.6.12** (plays together with 1.6.6 to 1.6.11). Its changes are in README_EDF6MultiSlot.txt in the ZIP.

To install, use one of these. Your settings are kept.
- Press **Update now** in EDF6 VR setting.exe.
- Run `Update_EDF6VR.bat`.
- Extract the ZIP over your game folder.
